Key Modules:
Leave Policies (/dashboard/organization/leave-policies):
Stores policy definitions in LeavePolicy schema: name, allocationType (Monthly/Yearly), totalLeaves, carryForward (maxLimit), probationRule, and encashable.
Leave Request & Approval (/dashboard/admin/leaves & Employee Portal):
When an employee applies for a multi-day date range, leaveService.js automatically checks the branch's weekly off days and company holidays, counting only actual working days as duration.
On approval: Updates the Attendance table for those dates to status: 'leave', debits the LeaveBalance, and posts a LeaveTransaction in the leave ledger.
Leave Ledger (/dashboard/leave-ledger):
Tracks credit and debit transactions for each employee with live remaining balance.
Payroll Processing (/dashboard/payroll/create):
Gathers month attendance counts: present, absent, leaves, weeklyOffWork, and overtime.
Computes daily rate: $\text{Daily Rate} = \frac{\text{Base Salary}}{\text{Total Month Days}}$ (or Working Days).
2. Critical Flaws & Discrepancies in the Current Setup
🔴 1. Payroll Deduction Bug on Paid Leaves
The Issue: In 

payrollCreating.jsx
, when adjustLeave is checked:
javascript
if (options.adjustLeave && prev.leaveDays > 0) {
  const adjusted = Math.min(options.adjustedLeaveCount, employeeleavebal, prev.leaveDays);
  if (adjusted > 0) {
    updatedDeductions.push({
      name: "Paid Leave Adjustment",
      amount: (adjusted * perDayRate).toFixed(2), // ❌ BUG: Deducting money for paid leaves!
    });
  }
}
Why this is wrong: A Paid Leave is a benefit already funded by the company. If an employee takes 2 paid leaves and has sufficient balance, their salary should be 100% full (₹0 deduction).
The Fix: Only Unpaid Leaves (LWP) and Unapproved Absents should trigger a salary deduction: $$\text{Leave Deduction} = \text{Unpaid Leaves (LWP)} \times \text{Daily Rate}$$ Paid leaves should only reduce the Leave Balance in the ledger, with ₹0 salary deduction.
⚠️ 2. Lack of Automatic Monthly Accrual Engine
The Issue: Policies define allocationType: "monthly", but there is currently no background cron scheduler crediting monthly quota (e.g. +1.0 CL on the 1st of every month). Balances currently require manual credit in the Leave Ledger.
Industry Standard: A scheduler automatically runs on the 1st of each month to credit the monthly allowance to all active, non-probation employees.
⚠️ 3. Attendance Status Does Not Distinguish Paid Leave vs Unpaid Leave (LWP)
The Issue: In server/services/leaveService.js, every approved leave marks attendance as status: 'leave'. If an employee has 0 leave balance and admin approves an emergency leave, it still marks status: 'leave'.
The Gap: The system needs a flag isPaid: true/false or leaveType: 'paid' | 'lwp' on the attendance record so the payroll engine knows instantly whether to pay or deduct without manual guessing.
3. Industry-Standard Leave & Payroll Architecture (Keka / Darwinbox / Zoho)
Feature	How Standard HRMS Handles It	Recommended for Good Nature EMS
Standard Leave Types	CL (Casual Leave), SL (Sick Leave), EL/PL (Earned/Privilege Leave), LWP (Leave Without Pay), Comp-Off (Weekly off work conversion).	Pre-configure standard types with allocation cycles (e.g. 1 CL/mo, 0.5 SL/mo).
Accrual (Credit)	Automatically credited on the 1st of each month or start of financial year.	Add an automated monthly accrual runner or 1-click batch credit.
Comp-Off (Weekly Off)	Working on Sunday/Weekly off can be credited to Comp-Off Leave Balance instead of direct cash bonus.	Allow employee/admin to choose: Cash Payout in Payroll OR Credit 1 Comp-Off Day.
Payroll Formula	$\text{Payable Days} = \text{Month Days} - (\text{LWP Days} + \text{Absent Days})$
$\text{Net Base} = \text{Payable Days} \times \text{Daily Rate}$	Align payroll math so Paid Leaves = Full Pay, only LWP/Absents = Deducted.
Half-Day Leaves	Support First Half (10:00–14:00) / Second Half (14:00–18:00) 0.5 day leaves.	Supported in attendance, just need UI dropdown in leave request.
Year-End Carry Forward / Encashment	At year end (March 31 / Dec 31), unused EL is either carried forward up to maxLimit or encashed at $\text{Days} \times \text{Daily Rate}$.	Add a Year-End Leave Settlement utility.
