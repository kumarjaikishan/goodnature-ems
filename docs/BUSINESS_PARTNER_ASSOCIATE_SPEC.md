# 3-Tier Hierarchy: Branch Partner, Business Partner, & Business Associate Specification

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Requirements

In **Good Nature EMS**, real estate operations and commissions follow an official 3-tier sales hierarchy:
1. **Branch / Branch Partner**: Highest regional / franchise head attached directly to a physical `Branch` (`branch.branchPartnerId`). Automatically earns a **0.5% Branch Commission override** on all booking collections sourced through that branch.
2. **Business Partner (BP)**: Senior sponsor attached to the Branch / Company (`sponsorId = null`). Identifier starts with `BP-` or `P/` (e.g., `BP-19/10/2627/001`).
3. **Business Associate (BA)**: Downline sponsor attached to a specific Business Partner (`sponsorId = partnerId`). Identifier starts with `BA-` or `A/` (e.g., `BA-19/10/2627/001`).
4. **Customer**: End customer (`PlotCustomer` / `User`), registered under a Business Associate or Business Partner (`customer.sponsorId = associateOrPartnerId`).

### Hierarchical Breakdown & Commission Entitlements:
- **Branch Partner (0.5% Fixed Override)**:
  - Receives automatic 0.5% branch override upon every receipt collection (`source: 'commission_branch'`) credited directly into their universal ledger (`ledgerId`).
  - Access to full branch performance metrics via the Business Developer portal (`/dashboard/business-developer`): Branch Turnover, total business sourced by all BPs and BAs in that branch, and direct link to their universal ledger statement.
  - Can be monitored and managed by Admin under **Organization -> Branches** (`/dashboard/organization/branches`) with assigned partner badge, and **Ledger -> Branch Partners** (`/dashboard/ledger/branch-partners`).
- **Business Partner (BP)**:
  - Direct commission according to the locked tenure slab matrix.
  - Team / differential override commission on collections generated by their enrolled Business Associates.
  - View all enrolled BAs, network customers, customer booking dues, and team performance reports.
- **Business Associate (BA)**:
  - Direct commission on self-sourced bookings.
  - View only self-enrolled customers, customer booking dues, and self-performance reports.

## 3. Commission Distribution & Ledger Posting Sequence

When a customer makes an installment or downpayment collection:
1. **Direct Associate Commission**: Calculated according to the booking's commission rate slab and credited to the sourcing sponsor (`source: 'commission_fixed'`).
2. **Business Partner Differential / Override**: If the booking was sourced by a BA, the differential between the BP rate and BA rate is credited to the parent Business Partner.
3. **Branch Partner 0.5% Override**:
   - The booking's `branchId` is inspected for an assigned `branchPartnerId`.
   - The system calculates $\text{Branch Override Amount} = \text{Collection Realized} \times 0.5\%$.
   - Automatically posts a double-entry Credit to the Branch Partner's universal ledger (`source: 'commission_branch'`) with reference to the booking and collection receipt.
   - Saves an audit record in `PlotSponsorCommission` with `commissionRole: 'BRANCH_OVERRIDE'`.
